World Time Zones Map

Real day/night terminator · every timezone at a glance

About

World Time Zones Map colors 400+ IANA timezones with their live UTC offset (DST-aware) and draws the real astronomical day/night terminator. Tap a city or land for its local time.

FAQ

Does the app collect any data?

No. It is fully offline — no network requests, no analytics, no tracking. Nothing ever leaves your device.

How do I change the language?

The UI follows your system language and supports 中文 / English / 日本語. Any other system language shows English.

Are the timezones DST-aware?

Yes. Each timezone's color reflects its current UTC offset including daylight saving time, and refreshes automatically.

Can I see another moment in time?

Yes — use the timeline to travel ±24 hours or ±365 days and preview any date/time, including DST transitions.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad running iOS 16.0 or later.
